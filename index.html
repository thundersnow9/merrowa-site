We make the waiting part of travel paperwork shorter.

Merrowa is an independent software company based in Oregon. Our product is Haya, which tells you the moment an interview appointment opens for Global Entry, NEXUS or SENTRI.

Haya

Getting approved for a Trusted Traveler program ends with an in-person interview, and at many enrollment centers the next open interview is months away. Appointments do come free when other people cancel, but each one is usually rebooked within minutes.

Haya checks the official U.S. Customs and Border Protection scheduler every five minutes and emails you when a slot opens at the centers you pick. Email alerts are free. Push and text alerts for near-term slots are part of a paid plan. You always book the appointment yourself, on the government's own site.

How we build

  • Public data only

    Haya reads the same public scheduling feed the official booking site uses. We publish what we learn from it too: Haya's interview wait times by enrollment center and a free JSON API are open to anyone.

  • No government credentials

    We never ask for your Trusted Traveler login or your application details, and we never book on your behalf.

  • Independent

    Merrowa is not affiliated with or endorsed by U.S. Customs and Border Protection or any other government agency.
