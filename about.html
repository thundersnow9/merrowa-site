About Merrowa

Merrowa Technologies LLC is a founder-run software company based in Oregon. We have one product, Haya.

Where it started

Merrowa's founder, Isaac Pato, needed a Global Entry interview for his newborn son so the family could keep traveling together. Every enrollment center nearby was booked for months. Cancelled appointments did appear, but they were gone again within minutes.

He wrote a small program to watch the official scheduler and tell him when something opened. It found the family an appointment within days. That program became Haya, and Merrowa is the company that runs it.

What we do now

Haya watches interview availability for Global Entry, NEXUS and SENTRI and alerts people when a slot opens at the enrollment centers they choose. The story of the product, its data sources and its methodology are on Haya's own About page.
